Our Story

From a two-person freelance team to a full studio

DevPlus+ began as two engineers building websites for local businesses out of a shared laptop. What we noticed early was simple: most clients weren't getting bad code, they were getting slow communication, missed deadlines, and half-finished handoffs. We built DevPlus+ to fix that — a studio where the people who scope your project are the same people who build it.

Today we're a distributed team of designers, engineers, and product strategists working with founders and internal teams across fintech, health, and B2B SaaS.
