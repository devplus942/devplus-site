Pricing FAQ

Common questions about how we quote

Why fixed price instead of hourly?

Fixed pricing forces us to scope carefully upfront, which protects you from open-ended billing and protects us from scope creep. If requirements change mid-project, we quote the delta separately rather than padding the invoice.

What happens if my project doesn't fit these tiers?

Most real projects fall between tiers. We use these as a starting reference on the scoping call, then send a written quote matched to your actual requirements.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes — standard terms are 40% at kickoff, 40% at midpoint demo, and 20% at launch. Enterprise engagements can be billed monthly against milestones.

Is hosting included?

We set up hosting and infrastructure under your own accounts so you retain full ownership and control of costs — we don't mark up hosting fees.
