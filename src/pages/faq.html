FAQ

Questions we hear before kickoff

Can't find what you're looking for? Reach out directly.

How long does a typical project take?

Marketing sites take about two weeks. Full web or mobile apps typically run six to ten weeks depending on scope, and we confirm a specific date before kickoff.

Do I own the code after launch?

Yes. Full source code, design files, and infrastructure access transfer to you at project handoff — there's no ongoing dependency on us unless you want a support retainer.

Can you work with our in-house team?

Yes, we regularly work alongside in-house engineers and designers as an embedded extension of the team, joining existing standups and sprint cycles.

What if I need changes after launch?

Every project includes a post-launch support window. After that, we offer monthly maintenance retainers or can quote changes individually.

Which industries have you worked in?

Primarily fintech, healthtech, logistics, and B2B SaaS, though our process transfers cleanly to most product types. See our portfolio for examples.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes, we're happy to sign your NDA before any detailed scoping conversation — just mention it when you reach out.

Do you offer fixed-price contracts?

Yes, most engagements are fixed price after scoping. See our pricing page for typical ranges.
